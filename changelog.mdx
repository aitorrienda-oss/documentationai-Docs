Replace the sample dates, version numbers, and sections with your own release history. You can also add links to related documentation pages, internal tickets, or migration notes if your workflow needs more detail.

<Expandable title="What to include in each entry" default-open="false">

A strong changelog entry usually includes a version label, a date, a short description, and grouped bullet points. If a change affects user workflows, call it out clearly so readers can understand the impact at a glance.

You can also add a short note when a release includes template updates, wording changes, or structural improvements.

</Expandable>
