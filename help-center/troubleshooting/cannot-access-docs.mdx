title: Cannot Access Documentation
description: Quick fixes for access or permission errors.
---

## Symptoms

- You see a "Permission Denied" error
- Cannot open or edit documents

## Causes

- Not signed in
- Missing access rights

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your login status">
    Log in at `https://aitorrienda.com/login` and try again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request access">
    Ask an administrator to add you to the project or share the document directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>
