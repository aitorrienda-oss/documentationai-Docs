---
title: Create Your First Document
description: A quick guide to making your first doc in Aitor Rienda.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new doc">
    Click `New Document` in your project dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your content">
    Enter a title and write your text. Use formatting for headings, lists, or code blocks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and share">
    Click `Save`. Use the `Share` button to send to teammates or copy a public link.
  </Step>
</Steps>
