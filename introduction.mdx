## Who this is for

This documentation works best for new users, teammates, and contributors who need a fast overview before diving deeper. You can use it to orient people, reduce repeated questions, and keep essential information in one place.

It also helps you standardize how you present product details. Instead of scattering explanations across messages or screenshots, you can keep the important context here and expand it over time.

## Getting started

Begin with the most common user goal, then expand into deeper sections as needed. A good documentation home page answers three questions right away: what this is, where to begin, and where to go next.

You can also reuse this structure for internal knowledge bases, client handoff docs, or product onboarding portals. Keep the content clear, concise, and easy to scan so readers can move through it quickly.
